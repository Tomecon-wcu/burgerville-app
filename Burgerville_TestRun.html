As a monopoly, you set your own price by choosing your quantity -- use marginal revenue vs. marginal cost to find the profit-maximizing point. Enter your own estimates in Section 1, choose how many farms to operate and how much to produce in Section 2, then build your firm's performance and welfare analysis in the light-green cells of Section 3. As the monopolist, you're not limited by a starting endowment the way a competitive firm is -- you're understood to be able to raise whatever capital it takes to buy up as much land as you want to corner the market, so choose your farm count based on what maximizes your profit, not on any budget constraint.
